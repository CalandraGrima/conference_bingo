      Your data

      Your card, your progress, your totals, your completed cards and your light/dark choice are stored on this device only, in your browser. Nothing you tap is sent anywhere, and there is no account and no login.

      We use GoatCounter to count visits and four anonymous milestones (a new player, a card started, a bingo, a full card), so we can show the combined totals above. It sets no cookies and does not store anything that identifies you. If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control, it is not loaded at all and the combined totals are not fetched.
